Refund Policy

SiteCheck NZ · Effective 12 October 2026

7-day refund

If SiteCheck NZ isn't right for you, email contact@lbpnz.nz within 7 days of payment and we will refund you in full. Include the email address you used at checkout. When we refund your pass, it ends, and you must stop using your unlock link.

After 7 days

After 7 days, payments are not refundable, and your pass continues until the end of its 12 months. Passes do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993. For example, you may be entitled to a remedy if the service is not provided with reasonable care and skill.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to your original payment method through Stripe, and usually arrive within 5–10 business days.
