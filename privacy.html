Privacy Policy

SiteCheck NZ · Effective 12 October 2026

Who we are

SiteCheck NZ is a trading name of the trustees of the MBP Trust. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 2020. Contact: contact@lbpnz.nz.

Your checklist records stay on your device

Your answers, photos, signatures and logos are stored only in the browser on your device. They are not sent to us, and we cannot see them. PDFs are created on your device.

What we collect when you pay

  • Payment details: Stripe collects your name, email address and card details to process your payment and send your tax invoice. We can see your payment record in Stripe, but never your full card number.
  • Your pass: your pass is a signed code that records your plan and expiry date. It contains no name or email, and is held in a cookie on your device and in your unlock link.
  • Messages: anything you send to us by email.

We do not run user accounts, and our site does not store a customer database.

Why we use it

We use your information to process payments and refunds, issue tax invoices, answer your questions, and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing emails.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the providers we need to run the service: Stripe for payments, Cloudflare for hosting and security, and our email provider. Some of these providers store data outside New Zealand. We use reputable providers with privacy safeguards comparable to New Zealand's. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep payment and invoice records for 7 years to meet tax law, and delete emails when they are no longer needed.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie that holds your pass, so paid checklists open on your device. Cloudflare may set security cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal information by emailing contact@lbpnz.nz. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
